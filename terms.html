6. Estimates and reminders are informational

AutoKeep calculates fuel use, costs, "next due" estimates, reminders, exchange rates, mileage reports and details read from receipts from what you enter and from public data. They can be incomplete or wrong, for example if a reading is missing or mistyped. They are for your information only and are not mechanical, safety, legal, tax or financial advice.

  • Follow your car maker's maintenance schedule and the advice of a qualified mechanic, and check deadlines on your official documents, such as insurance and inspection.
  • Reminders are local notifications and may not arrive, for example if notifications are turned off or your device is off.
  • Check details read from a receipt before you save them. Check mileage reports before you hand them to an employer or a tax authority.
  • Exchange rates are reference rates and can differ from the rate your bank or card charged.
  • Do not use AutoKeep while driving. Start or end trips before you set off, when parked, or ask a passenger.
