Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

This policy explains how AutoKeep ("AutoKeep", "the app"), developed by Victor Dovgaliuc ("we", "us"), handles information. The short version: we don't collect it.

In short
  • We do not collect, store or receive any personal data. We have no servers and cannot see what you enter.
  • The app has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and uses no third-party SDKs.
  • Your cars, entries, documents and photos are stored on your iPhone. With AutoKeep Plus you can choose to sync them through your own private iCloud, which we cannot access.
  • Receipts and documents are read on your iPhone and are never uploaded.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Information stored on your device

To do its job, AutoKeep saves what you enter in the app's private storage on your device:

  • your cars and their details (for example name, make, model, year, number plate, VIN, insurer, policy number, insurance renewal and inspection dates, tank and battery size, notes and a photo of the car);
  • fill-ups and charges, repairs and their items, expenses, trips and reminders;
  • documents and receipt scans you add, and the names you reuse (stations, garages, repair items, categories);
  • your settings, such as units, currency, appearance and tips.

This data does not leave your device through AutoKeep, except in the cases described below that you choose to use. We have no servers and cannot see it. It is removed when you delete the app. Depending on your device settings, it may be included in your own iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone, which are governed by Apple.

iCloud sync (optional, AutoKeep Plus)

If you turn on iCloud sync, AutoKeep stores a copy of your data in the private database of your own iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit, so it appears on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. This private database belongs to your Apple Account: we cannot access, read or recover it. Apple stores and protects it under the iCloud Terms and Conditions and the Apple Privacy Policy. iCloud sync is off unless you turn it on, and you can turn it off at any time in AutoKeep's Settings.

Exchange rates (AutoKeep Plus)

When you have AutoKeep Plus and log an amount in a currency other than your home currency, the app downloads the day's reference exchange rates from the public Frankfurter service (frankfurter.app), which publishes European Central Bank data. The request contains only currency codes (for example "EUR"). No amounts, entries, identifiers or other personal data are sent. As with any request on the internet, the service can see your device's IP address; we don't operate it and receive nothing from it. Rates are kept on your device for about a day so they don't have to be downloaded again. Without Plus, you type rates yourself and no request is made.

Receipt and document scanning

When you scan a receipt, invoice or document, the camera image is processed on your device. Text is read with Apple's Vision framework and, on devices that support it, Apple's on-device language model (the Foundation Models framework) to find details such as the place, date, items and total. All of this happens on your iPhone: scans and the text read from them are never uploaded, and neither we nor anyone else receive them. Scans you keep are stored with your data as described above.

In-app purchases and subscriptions

AutoKeep Plus is sold through the App Store and purchases are processed by Apple. We never receive your name, Apple Account, payment card or billing details. The app receives a signed confirmation from Apple that a purchase succeeded and checks it on your device with Apple's StoreKit to unlock Plus; no server of ours is involved. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your device's Settings under your name › Subscriptions. Apple's handling of this information is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

Permissions

AutoKeep asks for a permission only when you first use the feature that needs it, and the rest of the app works without it:

  • Camera: to scan documents and receipts.
  • Photos: to pick a photo of your car or import a document. iOS gives AutoKeep only the photos you pick.
  • Notifications: for the reminders and alerts described below.

Notifications

All notifications are local: they are scheduled on your device, no push server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere. AutoKeep uses them for the reminders you set up, documents that are about to expire, and, if you start a free trial, a reminder 2 days before the trial ends. You can turn them off in AutoKeep's Settings or in your device's Settings.

Widgets and Live Activity

AutoKeep's widgets and the trip Live Activity show a summary of your data, such as this month's spending, what is coming up and the running trip. The app shares this summary with them through a container on your device (an App Group) that only AutoKeep's own components can read. It never leaves your device. Widgets and Live Activities can be visible on your Lock Screen; you can remove them or turn the Lock Screen timer off for a trip.

Backups and exports

When you create a backup or export a report, AutoKeep creates the file on your device and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to save or send it, for example Files, iCloud Drive or email. These files contain your data, including attached documents in backups, so keep them somewhere safe.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Delete any entry, car or document in the app and it is removed from your device.
  • Delete the app to remove everything AutoKeep stores on that device.
  • With iCloud sync on, deletions in the app are synced to your iCloud as well. To remove AutoKeep's data from iCloud entirely, use iCloud storage management in your device's Settings (your name › iCloud).
  • Backup and export files you created, and your own device backups, are yours to keep or delete.

We hold no copy of your data, so we cannot recover it for you.

Children

AutoKeep is not directed at children. Because the app does not collect personal data from anyone, it does not collect personal data from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region).

Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR give you rights over personal data that a company holds about you. Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. You stay in control in the app: you can export everything with a backup and delete anything at any time.

Changes to this policy

If AutoKeep's data practices change, for example if a future version adds a feature that needs to collect data, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released, and mention the change in the release notes.
