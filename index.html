Need help?

Email victordov@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, the AutoKeep version (Settings › About) and what you were doing when the problem happened. AutoKeep has no accounts, so there is nothing to look up on our side: details and screenshots help a lot. We read every message.

What AutoKeep is

AutoKeep is an iPhone app that keeps track of what your car costs and what it needs. Log fill-ups and charges, repairs with their parts and labour, parking, tolls and other expenses, and trips. AutoKeep works out your fuel use and cost per km, and reminds you about oil changes, tyres, insurance and inspection before they are due.

  • Private by design. No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your data stays on your iPhone.
  • Free for one car, with all logging, trips, reminders, documents, backup and export.
  • AutoKeep Plus PLUS adds unlimited cars, receipts that fill themselves in, stats for any range, the business mileage report, automatic currency rates, iCloud sync, widgets and comparing your cars.

Frequently asked questions

How do I switch between kilometres and miles, or litres and gallons?

Open Settings (the gear on Home) › Units. Choose the distance unit (km or miles), the fuel unit (litres or gallons) and how fuel use is shown (L/100 km, km/L, or MPG with miles and gallons). For a single entry, for example a fill-up abroad, tap the small unit button inside the field. Switching units never changes what you logged: AutoKeep converts for display.

I paid in another currency. How does AutoKeep handle it?

Set your home currency once in Settings › Money; all totals and stats use it. When you pay in another currency, tap the currency button on the amount and pick the currency you paid in. AutoKeep keeps the original amount and the rate, and converts the amount to your home currency.

  • With AutoKeep Plus, today's rate is filled in for you, using the European Central Bank's daily reference rates (through frankfurter.app). Only the currency codes are sent; see the Privacy Policy.
  • Without Plus, type the rate yourself, for example from your bank statement.

Reference rates can differ slightly from the rate your bank charged.

I didn't note the odometer. Can I still save?

Yes. The odometer is optional everywhere: fill-ups, charges, repairs, expenses and trips. AutoKeep never guesses a reading. The entry is saved with an Add km tag in History; tap it any time to add the reading. Fuel use is worked out between fill-ups that have a reading, and fuel from fill-ups without one is added to the next stretch, so nothing is lost from your totals. The more readings you add, the more detailed your stats get.

How do trips work?

Tap Trip on Home to start one. Pick a category such as Business or Personal; the name and odometer are optional. While a trip is running, new fill-ups, repairs and expenses are counted in it (each one has a Count it in this trip switch). End the trip in the app, or with one tap on the Lock Screen, and add the km later. Forgot to start one? Use Log a past trip. Deleting a trip never deletes its fill-ups or expenses; they stay in History.

I ended a trip by mistake. Can I resume it?

Yes, within 24 hours, as long as it is the car's latest trip and you haven't started a newer one. Tap Resume trip on Home, or open the trip and tap Resume trip. The timer carries on from the original start, so the time it was stopped counts as part of the trip, and AutoKeep offers to add anything you logged while it was stopped. For older trips, open the trip and change its start or end time instead.

How does the free trial work?

The yearly plan starts with a free week for new subscribers (Apple decides who is eligible; usually one free trial per Apple Account). If notifications are on, AutoKeep reminds you 2 days before the trial ends. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends and you won't be charged. Otherwise the yearly subscription starts and renews every year until you cancel. The monthly plan renews every month. Lifetime is a one-time purchase with no renewals. Prices are shown in the app, in your currency, before you buy.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In AutoKeep, open Settings › Manage subscription. Or, on your iPhone, open Settings › your name › Subscriptions › AutoKeep Plus › Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next charge. You keep Plus until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

What happens to my data if Plus ends?

Nothing is deleted. Everything you logged stays. If you have more than one car, you pick the car you keep logging on (you can change it later in My car); your other cars stay viewable and exportable. Trips, reminders, backup and export keep working.

I bought Plus but don't see it. How do I restore purchases?

Open Settings in AutoKeep and tap Restore purchases (it is also on the Plus screen). Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you bought with. If a purchase is waiting for approval, for example with Ask to Buy, Plus turns on once it is approved.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, and we can't see or refund payments. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I move to a new phone?

  • iCloud sync PLUS: sign in to both iPhones with the same Apple Account and turn on iCloud sync in AutoKeep (Settings › Your data). Your data appears on the new iPhone once it has synced.
  • Backup file (free): on the old iPhone, open Settings › Backup & restore, create a backup and save it to Files or iCloud Drive, or AirDrop it. On the new iPhone, install AutoKeep, open the same screen and restore the file. Restoring the same backup twice never creates duplicates.
  • Whole-phone transfer: moving with Quick Start, or restoring your iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup, brings AutoKeep's data along too.

Then tap Restore purchases on the new iPhone to bring Plus back.

Where are the widgets?

Touch and hold your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap Edit › Add Widget and search for AutoKeep. Widgets are part of AutoKeep Plus. Open AutoKeep once after installing so the widgets have something to show.

Where is my data stored? Can you see it?

Your data is stored on your iPhone. There are no accounts and no servers, so we can't see it. With iCloud sync it is also kept in your own private iCloud, which only your devices can read. Receipts and documents are read on your iPhone and never uploaded. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Not mechanical or financial advice

Stats, estimates and reminders help you keep track; they are calculated from what you enter. Follow your car maker's maintenance schedule and a qualified mechanic, and check the deadlines on your insurance and inspection documents. Please don't use AutoKeep while driving.
